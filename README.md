# CIE AS Physics — Topics 4.1, 4.2 and 5

Interactive study notes and 43 flashcards for [Cambridge International AS Physics (9702)](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-physics-9702/), 2025–2027 syllabus.

**[Open the notes and flashcards](https://jz-xbian.github.io/cie-as-physics-4-and-5/)** — or download `index.html` and open it in a browser; it works fully offline.

## What's included

- **Notes** — concise teaching pages with diagrams and worked examples
  - **4.1** Turning effects of forces (centre of gravity, moment, couple, torque of a couple)
  - **4.2** Equilibrium of forces (principle of moments, no resultant force or torque, vector triangle)
  - **5.1** Energy conservation (work, conservation of energy, efficiency, power)
  - **5.2** Gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy
- **Flashcards** — 43 cards covering key facts, definitions, equations and syllabus-required derivations. Filter by topic, flip to recall, and mark cards as known. Progress is stored in your browser.

Definitions follow the official syllabus wording. Equations such as \(W = Fs\cos\theta\), \(E_K = \frac12 mv^2\), \(\Delta E_P = mg\Delta h\), \(P = Fv\) and moment \(= Fd\) are **not** on the exam data sheet and must be recalled.
