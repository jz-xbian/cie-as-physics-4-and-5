4.1 Turning effects of forces力的转动效应

You should be able to:你应当能够：
  1. understand that the weight of an object may be taken as acting at a single point known as its centre of gravity理解物体的重量可视为作用在称为重心的单一点上
  2. define and apply the moment of a force定义并运用力矩
  3. understand that a couple is a pair of forces that acts to produce rotation only理解力偶是一对只产生转动的力
  4. define and apply the torque of a couple定义并运用力偶矩

Centre of gravity重心

Definition — memorise this定义 — 请记牢

The centre of gravity of an object is the point at which its entire weight may be taken as acting.物体的重心是其全部重量可视为作用的那一点。

For calculations, you can replace the object’s distributed weight with a single arrow W = mg drawn downward from the centre of gravity (C.G.). For a uniform, regular object the C.G. lies at the geometric centre. For an irregular object it can be found experimentally by hanging the object from two different points and seeing where the vertical plumb lines cross.计算时，可用从重心（C.G.）竖直向下的一个箭头 W = mg 来代替物体分布的重量。形状规则的均匀物体，重心在几何中心。不规则物体可用实验确定：分别从两个不同点悬挂，看两条铅垂线的交点。

In a uniform gravitational field (near the Earth’s surface) the centre of gravity coincides with the centre of mass.在匀强重力场中（地球表面附近），重心与质心重合。

Moment of a force力矩

Definition — memorise this定义 — 请记牢

The moment of a force about a point is the product of the force and the perpendicular distance from that point to the line of action of the force.力对某点的力矩，等于该力与从该点到力的作用线的垂直距离（力臂）的乘积。

moment = F × d⊥ unit: N m  ·  direction: clockwise or anticlockwise单位：N m · 方向：顺时针或逆时针

The line of action is the infinite straight line along which the force points. The distance you need is not necessarily the distance to the point of application — it is the shortest (perpendicular) gap from the pivot to that line.作用线是力所沿的那条无限长直线。你需要的距离不一定是到施力点的距离 — 而是从支点到该作用线的最短（垂直）间隔。

If a force F is applied at a distance r from the pivot, and the angle between the force and the line from the pivot to the point of application is θ, then若力 F 作用在距支点 r 处，且力与“支点到施力点连线”之间的夹角为 θ，则

moment = F r sin θ because  d⊥ = r sin θ因为 d⊥ = r sin θ

A force through the pivot (d⊥ = 0), or a force parallel to the line from the pivot (θ = 0° or 180°), has zero moment: it does not tend to turn the object.力的作用线通过支点（d⊥ = 0），或力与“支点到施力点连线”平行（θ = 0° 或 180°）时，力矩为零：该力不会使物体转动。

Worked example例题

Moment with a force at an angle成角度的力的力矩

A force of 40 N is applied to a door at 0.80 m from the hinge. The force is at 30° to the door, in the plane of the door’s swing. Calculate the moment about the hinge.一门上，距铰链 0.80 m 处施加 40 N 的力。该力与门成 30°，且在门转动的平面内。求对铰链的力矩。

The perpendicular distance from the hinge to the line of action is d⊥ = 0.80 × sin 30° = 0.40 m.从铰链到作用线的垂直距离为 d⊥ = 0.80 × sin 30° = 0.40 m。

moment = F × d⊥ = 40 × 0.40 = 16 N m (tending to open the door).力矩 = F × d⊥ = 40 × 0.40 = 16 N m（使门打开的方向）。

Exam trap考试陷阱

The unit of moment is N m, never the joule. A joule is also 1 N m, but a joule is a unit of energy. A moment is a turning effect, not a form of energy — writing “J” loses the mark.力矩的单位是 N m，绝不是焦耳。焦耳虽然也等于 1 N m，但焦耳是能量的单位。力矩是转动效应，不是一种能量 — 写成 “J” 会失分。

Couples and the torque of a couple力偶与力偶矩

Definitions — memorise these定义 — 请记牢

A couple is a pair of forces that acts to produce rotation only. The two forces are equal in magnitude, opposite in direction, and have parallel lines of action that are separated (they are not in the same straight line).力偶是一对只产生转动的力。这两个力大小相等、方向相反，作用线平行且彼此分开（不在同一直线上）。

The torque of a couple is the product of one of the forces and the perpendicular distance between the two lines of action.力偶矩等于其中一个力与两条作用线之间垂直距离的乘积。

torque of a couple = F × d unit: N m  ·  same about every point单位：N m · 对任意点都相同

Because the forces are equal and opposite, the resultant force is zero, so the object’s centre of mass does not accelerate. There is still a turning effect, which is why a couple produces rotation only. A useful property: the torque of a given couple is the same no matter which point you take moments about.由于两力大小相等、方向相反，合力为零，所以物体质心不会加速。但仍有转动效应，因此力偶只产生转动。一个有用的性质：给定力偶的力偶矩，无论对哪一点取矩都相同。

Examples: turning a steering wheel, a screwdriver, or the two hands on a tap.例子：转动方向盘、螺丝刀，或用双手拧水龙头。

4.2 Equilibrium of forces力的平衡

You should be able to:你应当能够：
  1. state and apply the principle of moments陈述并运用力矩原理
  2. understand that, when there is no resultant force and no resultant torque, a system is in equilibrium理解当合力为零且合力矩为零时，系统处于平衡
  3. use a vector triangle to represent coplanar forces in equilibrium用矢量三角形表示处于平衡的共面力

The principle of moments力矩原理

Definition — memorise this定义 — 请记牢

The principle of moments: for a system in equilibrium, the sum of the clockwise moments about any point is equal to the sum of the anticlockwise moments about the same point.力矩原理：对于处于平衡的系统，对任意一点的顺时针力矩之和，等于对同一点的逆时针力矩之和。

Σ clockwise moments = Σ anticlockwise momentsΣ 顺时针力矩 = Σ 逆时针力矩 about the same point

You may take moments about any convenient point. A standard trick is to choose a point on the line of action of an unknown force (often a support reaction). That force then has zero moment, and it drops out of the equation.可以对任意方便的点取矩。常用技巧是选在未知力（常常是支点反力）的作用线上的一点。该力的力矩便为零，从而从方程中消去。

Worked example例题

A uniform beam on a pivot支点上的均匀梁

A uniform beam AB has length 2.4 m and weight 180 N. It rests horizontally on a pivot 0.80 m from A, with a load W at end A, and is in equilibrium. Find W and the support reaction R.均匀梁 AB 长 2.4 m，重量 180 N。它水平搁在距 A 端 0.80 m 的支点上，A 端有载荷 W，系统处于平衡。求 W 和支点反力 R。

The beam’s weight acts at its centre, 1.2 m from A, so 0.40 m to the right of the pivot.梁的重量作用在其中心，距 A 1.2 m，亦即在支点右侧 0.40 m。

Take moments about the pivot.对支点取矩。

Anticlockwise: W × 0.80.   Clockwise: 180 × 0.40 = 72 N m.逆时针：W × 0.80。顺时针：180 × 0.40 = 72 N m。

Principle of moments: W × 0.80 = 72 ⇒ W = 90 N.力矩原理：W × 0.80 = 72 ⇒ W = 90 N。

No resultant force (vertical): R = 90 + 180 = 270 N upward.竖直方向合力为零：R = 90 + 180 = 270 N，方向向上。

The two conditions for equilibrium平衡的两个条件

Statement — memorise this陈述 — 请记牢

A system is in equilibrium when there is no resultant force and no resultant torque (moment).当系统合力为零且合力矩为零时，系统处于平衡。

Translational equilibrium平动平衡

No resultant force: ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0. The centre of mass does not accelerate (it is at rest or moves at constant velocity).合力为零：ΣFx = 0 且 ΣFy = 0。质心不加速（静止或匀速运动）。

Rotational equilibrium转动平衡

No resultant torque: Σ moments = 0 about any point. The object does not have an angular acceleration (it is not starting to spin, or it rotates at a constant rate).合力矩为零：对任意点 Σ moments = 0。物体没有角加速度（不会开始旋转，或以恒定角速度旋转）。

Both conditions must hold. A couple is a reminder that “no resultant force” is not enough on its own — a couple has zero resultant force but is not in equilibrium, because there is a resultant torque.两个条件都必须满足。力偶提醒我们：仅有“合力为零”是不够的 — 力偶的合力为零，但并不处于平衡，因为存在合力矩。

The vector triangle矢量三角形

If three coplanar forces act on a system that is in equilibrium, the forces can be represented by a closed vector triangle. Draw the three force vectors tip-to-tail. Because the resultant is zero, the tip of the last vector meets the tail of the first — the triangle closes.若三个共面力作用在处于平衡的系统上，这些力可用闭合的矢量三角形表示。把三个力矢量首尾相接画出。因为合力为零，最后一个矢量的箭头会接到第一个的箭尾 — 三角形闭合。

Each side is parallel to one of the forces and has a length proportional to that force’s magnitude. You can then use scale drawing, or trigonometry (sine or cosine rule) on the triangle, to find unknown forces or angles.每条边与其中一个力平行，长度与该力的大小成正比。然后可用按比例作图，或对三角形用三角学（正弦或余弦定理）求未知力或角度。

For more than three coplanar forces in equilibrium, the same idea gives a closed vector polygon.超过三个共面力处于平衡时，同样的思路给出闭合的矢量多边形。

Exam tip考试提示

If you are asked to “draw a vector triangle”, do it as a separate diagram, not just the free-body arrows on the object. Label each side with the force’s name and, if known, its magnitude. Mark the angles. State the scale if it is a scale drawing.如果题目要求“画出矢量三角形”，要画成单独的图，不要只画物体上的受力箭头。每边标上力的名称，已知则标大小。标出角度。若是按比例作图，要写明比例尺。

5 Work, energy and power功、能量和功率

The syllabus assumes you already know the forms of energy and energy transfers from IGCSE / O Level. Topic 5 is in two parts: 5.1 Energy conservation (work, conservation, efficiency, power) and 5.2 Gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy.考纲假定你已从 IGCSE / O Level 掌握各种形式的能量及能量转移。主题 5 分两部分：5.1 能量守恒（功、守恒、效率、功率）和 5.2 重力势能和动能。

5.1 Energy conservation能量守恒

Work done功

Definition — memorise this定义 — 请记牢

Work done by a force is the product of the force and the displacement in the direction of the force.力所做的功，等于该力与沿力的方向的位移的乘积。

W = F s cos θ unit: J  ·  1 J = 1 N m  ·  θ is the angle between F and s单位：J · 1 J = 1 N m · θ 是 F 与 s 之间的夹角

Work is a scalar. If the force is parallel to the displacement, θ = 0° and W = F s. If the force is perpendicular to the displacement (θ = 90°), cos 90° = 0 so W = 0 — a useful fact for circular motion and for the normal contact force on a level surface. A force opposite to the motion (θ = 180°) does negative work: the object loses energy.功是标量。若力与位移平行，θ = 0°，则 W = F s。若力与位移垂直（θ = 90°），cos 90° = 0，故 W = 0 — 这对圆周运动以及水平面上的支持力很有用。与运动方向相反的力（θ = 180°）做负功：物体失去能量。

Worked example例题

Work done at an angle成角度做的功

A crate is pulled 6.0 m along a floor by a force of 50 N at 25° to the horizontal. Calculate the work done by this force.用 50 N、与水平成 25° 的力，把木箱沿地面拉动 6.0 m。求该力所做的功。

W = F s cos θ = 50 × 6.0 × cos 25° = 272 J (3 significant figures).W = F s cos θ = 50 × 6.0 × cos 25° = 272 J（三位有效数字）。

Principle of conservation of energy能量守恒定律

Definition — memorise this定义 — 请记牢

The principle of conservation of energy: energy cannot be created or destroyed. It can be transferred from one form to another, but the total energy of a closed system remains constant.能量守恒定律：能量不能被创造或消灭。它可以从一种形式转移为另一种形式，但封闭系统的总能量保持不变。

In a mechanics problem this usually means that a loss in one form equals a gain in others. If there is no air resistance and no friction, gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy simply swap. If resistive forces act, some energy is transferred to thermal energy (internal energy of the object and the surroundings). The total is still constant; it is just no longer all mechanical.在力学问题中，这通常意味着一种形式减少的能量等于其他形式增加的能量。若没有空气阻力和摩擦，重力势能与动能只是互相转化。若存在阻力，部分能量会转移为热能（物体及周围的内能）。总量仍然守恒，只是不再全部是机械能。

Efficiency效率

Definition — memorise this定义 — 请记牢

The efficiency of a system is the ratio of useful energy output from the system to the total energy input. The same ratio can be written with power, because power is energy per unit time.系统的效率是系统有用能量输出与总能量输入之比。同一比值也可用功率来写，因为功率是单位时间的能量。

efficiency = useful energy output / total energy input效率 = 有用能量输出 / 总能量输入 or   useful power output / total power input或：有用功率输出 / 总功率输入

Efficiency is a ratio: a number between 0 and 1. It is often written as a percentage (multiply by 100%). Real machines always have efficiency less than 1, because some input energy is wasted, usually as thermal energy.效率是一个比值：介于 0 与 1 之间的数。也常写成百分数（乘以 100%）。实际机器的效率总是小于 1，因为部分输入能量被浪费，通常变为热能。

Worked example例题

Efficiency from power用功率求效率

A motor has an input power of 400 W and a useful output power of 280 W. Find its efficiency.一台电动机的输入功率为 400 W，有用输出功率为 280 W。求它的效率。

efficiency = useful power output / total power input = 280 / 400 = 0.70 (or 70%).效率 = 有用功率输出 / 总功率输入 = 280 / 400 = 0.70（或 70%）。

Power功率

Definition — memorise this定义 — 请记牢

Power is the work done per unit time (the rate of doing work, or the rate of energy transfer).功率是单位时间内做的功（做功的快慢，或能量转移的快慢）。

P = W / t unit: W  ·  1 W = 1 J s−1单位：W · 1 W = 1 J s⁻¹
Derivation you can be asked for — P = Fv可能要求你写出的推导 — P = Fv

If a constant force F moves an object a distance s in the direction of the force in time t,若恒力 F 在时间 t 内，使物体沿力的方向移动距离 s，

P = W / t = (F s) / t = F (s / t) = F v,

where v is the speed in the direction of the force. If F and v are at an angle θ, then P = F v cos θ.其中 v 是沿力方向的速率。若 F 与 v 成夹角 θ，则 P = F v cos θ。

Worked example例题

Power at constant speed匀速时的功率

A boat’s engine provides a driving force of 1.5 kN at a constant speed of 8.0 m s−1. Calculate the output power.一艘船的发动机提供 1.5 kN 的驱动力，船以 8.0 m s⁻¹ 的恒定速率行驶。求输出功率。

P = F v = 1.5 × 103 × 8.0 = 1.2 × 104 W (12 kW).P = F v = 1.5 × 103 × 8.0 = 1.2 × 104 W（12 kW）。

5.2 Gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy重力势能和动能

Gravitational potential energy重力势能

Formula — recall and use公式 — 需记忆并运用

In a uniform gravitational field (near the Earth’s surface), the change in gravitational potential energy is在匀强重力场中（地球表面附近），重力势能的变化为

ΔEP = mgΔh unit: J  ·  g = 9.81 m s−2 (from the data sheet)单位：J · g = 9.81 m s⁻²（来自数据表）
Derivation from W = F s — required by the syllabus由 W = F s 推导 — 考纲要求

To raise a mass m slowly through a vertical height Δh at constant speed, the applied upward force equals the weight mg.要以恒定速率把质量 m 缓慢升高竖直高度 Δh，向上的施加力等于重量 mg。

Work done by this force: W = F s = mg × Δh.该力做的功：W = F s = mg × Δh。

That work is stored as gravitational potential energy, so ΔEP = mg Δh.这份功被储存为重力势能，因此 ΔEP = mg Δh。

This holds only in a uniform field, where g is constant. (A different formula, not needed at AS, is used at large distances from the Earth.)这只在 g 为常量的匀强场中成立。（远离地球时要用另一个公式，AS 不要求。）

Worked example例题

Calculating ΔEP计算 ΔEP

A student of mass 55 kg climbs a vertical height of 12 m. Calculate the gain in gravitational potential energy. Take g = 9.81 m s−2.一名质量为 55 kg 的学生竖直攀升 12 m。求重力势能的增加。取 g = 9.81 m s−2。

ΔEP = mg Δh = 55 × 9.81 × 12 = 6.47 × 103 J.ΔEP = mg Δh = 55 × 9.81 × 12 = 6.47 × 103 J。

Kinetic energy动能

EK = ½ m v2 unit: J  ·  must be recalled — not on the data sheet单位：J · 必须记住 — 数据表上没有
Derivation from the equations of motion — required by the syllabus由运动方程推导 — 考纲要求

An object of mass m starts from rest (u = 0) and is accelerated by a constant force F through a displacement s, reaching speed v.质量为 m 的物体由静止出发（u = 0），在恒力 F 作用下通过位移 s，达到速率 v。

Newton’s second law: F = ma.牛顿第二定律：F = ma。

Equation of motion: v2 = u2 + 2as = 2as, so s = v2 / 2a.运动方程：v2 = u2 + 2as = 2as，所以 s = v2 / 2a。

Work done by the force: W = F s = (ma)(v2 / 2a) = ½ m v2.该力做的功：W = F s = (ma)(v2 / 2a) = ½ m v2。

This work equals the kinetic energy gained, so EK = ½ m v2.这份功等于获得的动能，因此 EK = ½ m v2。

If the object already has speed u, the same steps give W = ½ m v2 − ½ m u2 = ΔEK. This is the work–energy idea: the work done by the resultant force equals the change in kinetic energy.若物体已有速率 u，同样步骤得到 W = ½ m v2 − ½ m u2 = ΔEK。这就是功能关系：合力做的功等于动能的变化。

Worked example例题

Energy transfer, with and without resistance有阻力与无阻力时的能量转移

An apple of mass 0.15 kg falls 3.0 m from rest. Take g = 9.81 m s−2.一个质量为 0.15 kg 的苹果从静止下落 3.0 m。取 g = 9.81 m s−2。

(a) If air resistance is negligible, find its speed just before it lands.(a) 若空气阻力可忽略，求落地前瞬间的速率。

(b) If it actually lands at 6.0 m s−1, how much energy has been transferred to thermal energy?(b) 若它实际以 6.0 m s⁻¹ 落地，有多少能量已转移为热能？

(a) Loss in EP = gain in EK:(a) EP 的减少 = EK 的增加：

mg Δh = ½ m v2 ⇒ v = √(2g Δh) = √(2 × 9.81 × 3.0) = 7.67 m s−1.mg Δh = ½ m v2 ⇒ v = √(2g Δh) = √(2 × 9.81 × 3.0) = 7.67 m s−1。

(b) Loss in EP = 0.15 × 9.81 × 3.0 = 4.41 J.(b) EP 减少 = 0.15 × 9.81 × 3.0 = 4.41 J。

Gain in EK = ½ × 0.15 × (6.0)2 = 2.70 J.EK 增加 = ½ × 0.15 × (6.0)2 = 2.70 J。

Energy transferred to thermal = 4.41 − 2.70 = 1.71 J.转移为热能的能量 = 4.41 − 2.70 = 1.71 J。

Gravitational potential energy重力势能ΔEP = mgΔh ★Uniform field only. Derive from W = Fs.仅适用于匀强场。由 W = Fs 推导。
Kinetic energy动能EK = ½ m v2 ★Derive from the equations of motion.由运动方程推导。
Before the exam考前注意

Use g = 9.81 m s−2 unless the question gives another value. State the point you are taking moments about. Always use the perpendicular distance. For energy, check whether resistive forces are mentioned — if they are, mg Δh is not equal to ½mv2.除非题目另给数值，否则取 g = 9.81 m s−2。写明你对哪一点取矩。始终使用垂直距离。对能量问题，要看是否提到阻力 — 若有，mg Δh 就不等于 ½mv2。
